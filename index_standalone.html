The Platform

One engine, assembled from five builds.

Adrian is the shared inference engine. Each lens is a domain skin over it:

Catalyze — biomedical evidence → advancement verdict (ALK+ NSCLC wedge). Multi-model consensus.
Equity Lens — AI responses → fairness verdict, grounded in the counterfactual invariance method (USPTO provisional #19/632,273).
Sendero — operational data → BUILD vs TRAINING verdict, using a two-level outlier test that runs locally.

The common thread: every lens distinguishes real signal from artifact — η² (Sendero), cross-model spread (Catalyze), invariance (Equity Lens) — and shows its work.
